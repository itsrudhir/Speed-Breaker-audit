## Overview

Unauthorized or improperly constructed speed breakers are a major cause of traffic congestion and road accidents, especially in rapidly urbanizing regions. Manual inspections are inefficient due to the large scale of the issue. **RoadHAWK** is an edge-centric, automated system that leverages smartphone accelerometer data to detect and analyze speed breakers in real time.

## Key Features

- 🚗 **Edge-Centric Processing:** Uses a vehicle-mounted smartphone accelerometer for real-time data collection.
- 🔍 **Automated Detection:** Identifies speed breakers and characterizes their temporal span.
- ⚠️ **Violation Estimation:** Determines potential violations in the design and placement of speed breakers.
- 📊 **High Accuracy:** Achieves an average classification accuracy of approx **90%** in test data.
## Methodology



1. **Data Collection:** Smartphone accelerometer data is used to sense variations in vehicle motion.
2. **Sensor Informatics Pipeline:** Filters out measurement noise and ego-noise to detect speed breakers accurately.
3. **Feature Extraction:** Analyzes temporal and spatial characteristics of detected bumps.
4. **Violation Detection:** Compares observed speed breaker properties with regulatory standards.

## Results

- ✅ Effective real-time detection of speed breakers.
- ✅ High accuracy in estimating speed breaker dimensions and placement violations.
- ✅ Scalable and deployable as a low-cost solution for smart road monitoring.
